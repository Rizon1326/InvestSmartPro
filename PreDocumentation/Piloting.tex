\section{Proposed Solution \& Technical Methodology}

\subsection{Proposed Solution}

\textbf{InvestSmart} is an AI-powered web platform designed to help Bangladeshi youth, students, and aspiring entrepreneurs learn, test, and validate business ideas through interactive simulations. The platform combines \textbf{real-time market insights, AI-driven guidance, and hands-on business practice} to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world entrepreneurship.

The solution provides:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Business Idea Simulator:} Users test business concepts in a virtual environment with realistic market conditions.
    
    \item \textbf{AI Business Mentor:} A generative AI assistant that provides step-by-step guidance on planning, budgeting, pricing, marketing, and risk assessment.
    
    \item \textbf{Market Feasibility Analyzer:} Automated evaluation of demand, competition, investment needs, and profit potential.
    
    \item \textbf{Learning Hub:} Short modules, case studies, and tutorials to strengthen practical business skills.
    
    \item \textbf{Progress \& Performance Tracking} – Analytics dashboards showing improvement across decisions and simulations.
\end{itemize}

InvestSmart empowers beginners to make informed decisions \textit{before} investing money, directly supporting youth empowerment, self-employment, and SDG-aligned development.

\subsection{Technical Methodology}

\subsubsection{ AI Model / Approach}

\paragraph{a. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Used for fetching relevant business cases, examples, and learning content.
    \item Combines vector similarity search with LLM generation to produce personalized recommendations and explanations.
    \item \textbf{Example Use-Cases:}
          \begin{itemize}
              \item AI Business Mentor retrieves topic-specific content from structured knowledge base.
              \item Business Idea Simulator fetches similar historical business cases to predict outcomes.
          \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{b. Large Language Models (LLM):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Models like GPT-4, Mistral, or LLaMA used for:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item Generating step-by-step guidance and actionable advice.
              \item Explaining complex business concepts in simple terms.
              \item Suggesting mini-case exercises or implementation examples.
          \end{itemize}
    \item Fine-tuned or prompt-engineered for domain-specific business knowledge.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{ System Design \& Technical Workflow}

\paragraph{Overall Flow:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{User Input Layer:} \\
          Collects business idea, budget, location, team experience, or learning preferences.
    
    \item \textbf{Data Preprocessing \& Storage:} \\
          Structured data: PostgreSQL (business cases, KPIs). \\
          Unstructured data: MongoDB or vector database (learning modules, case studies).
    
    \item \textbf{AI Processing Layer:} \\
          RAG pipeline: Retrieve relevant content or past business cases. \\
          LLM: Generate personalized insights, learning guidance, or scenario predictions. \\
          Predictive ML models: Compute KPIs, risk scores, and feasibility metrics.
    
    \item \textbf{Simulation / Scenario Engine:} \\
          Evaluate multiple "what-if" scenarios for business ideas. \\
          Generate outputs like projected revenue, risk assessment, and success probability.
    
    \item \textbf{Learning \& Guidance Layer:} \\
          AI Mentor provides step-by-step explanations, practical examples, and gamified exercises.
    
    \item \textbf{Analytics \& Dashboard:} \\
          Track user progress, learning outcomes, and simulation performance. \\
          Visualize improvement and recommendations via charts and dashboards.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{ Datasets}

\paragraph{a. Internal / Proprietary Datasets:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Structured business case experiments, KPI results, success/failure scenarios.
    \item User interaction and learning progress data (for analytics and model feedback).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{b. External Datasets:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Market trends, competitor data, and economic indicators via web scraping (BeautifulSoup, Scrapy) or APIs (Google Trends, World Bank, Kaggle datasets).
    \item Business tutorials, books, and online resources for RAG-based learning content.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{c. Data Processing:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Text data $\rightarrow$ cleaned, tokenized, embedded into vector space for RAG.
    \item Numeric/market data $\rightarrow$ normalized and structured for predictive modeling.
\end{itemize}

\section{Scope and Pilot Implementation}

\subsection{Scope}

InvestSmart focuses on helping Bangladeshi youth learn, test, and validate business ideas using AI-driven tools. The project scope includes:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Business Idea Simulator:} Virtual testing of business ideas with cost, demand, risk, and competition factors.
    \item \textbf{AI Business Mentor:} Step-by-step guidance on planning, budgeting, pricing, and marketing using RAG + LLMs.
    \item \textbf{Market Feasibility Analyzer:} Automated scoring of demand, competition, investment needs, and profitability.
    \item \textbf{Learning Hub:} Short modules and case studies that build practical business skills.
    \item \textbf{Progress Dashboard:} Tracks user improvement and decision quality.
    \item \textbf{Target Users:} Students, young entrepreneurs, and unemployed youth seeking self-employment.
    \item \textbf{Technology Coverage:} RAG for retrieval, LLMs for guidance, ML models for risk and feasibility prediction.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Pilot Implementation}

The pilot will include a small set of core features to test the basic usefulness of InvestSmart.

\paragraph{Pilot Features}

\paragraph{Business Idea Input}
Users add a business idea with basic cost and price info.

\paragraph{Basic Simulation}
Shows estimated profit and risk level.

\paragraph{AI Mentor (Simple Tips)}
Gives short suggestions on pricing, cost, and marketing.

\paragraph{Feasibility Score}
Provides a basic rating: High / Medium / Low.

\paragraph{Small Learning Section}
Only 2–3 quick lessons on pricing, customers, and risk.

\paragraph{Simple Progress View}
Shows number of simulations and completed lessons.
